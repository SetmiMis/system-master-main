# Media production checklist

The site works today with catalogue images and a still-image intro. These items turn it into the finished brand film. None of this footage exists yet; do not use stock or generated footage presented as SETMI INDIA's own.

## Footage to record (real, on-location)
- [ ] The Old Lajpat Rai Market shopfront (167-168), exterior, day, 10 s steady shot.
- [ ] Shop interior: shelves of connectors and cables, slow dolly, 15 s.
- [ ] Hands picking and handling products (BNC, HDMI jointer, circular connector), 10 s each.
- [ ] Macro shots, 4K, black or dark background, slow rotation or slider: BNC, 17mm waterproof circular connector, HDMI splitter, a coiled audio cable, a battery clip. 8 to 10 s each.
- [ ] Staff helping a customer at the counter (with the customer's written consent), 15 s.
- [ ] Packing and dispatch, if it happens in-house, 10 s.
- [ ] Logo sting: the SETMI INDIA logo on dark charcoal, clean, 5 s.

## Photography
- [ ] Clean product photos on a neutral dark background, no baked-in text, 2000 px square, for every category. The current catalogue images contain marketing text and are cropped to hide it.
- [ ] A photo for Sockets & Plugs (currently an intentional placeholder).

## Audio
- [ ] Narration script approved, then recorded by a real voice (English; Hindi optional). Text is the intro captions plus the section copy.
- [ ] Optional ambient bed, licensed.

## Delivery specs
- MP4 H.264, 1920x1080, 24 or 25 fps, 6 to 10 Mbps, AAC audio. Intro 30 to 60 s, under 15 MB. Provide a 1280x720 mobile version and a poster JPG (1920x1080).
- WebVTT captions for each video with matching timing. Host on HTTPS, set in `/admin`.

## Approvals still required
- [ ] Wording of any "leading importer" or similar claim (not used on the site).
- [ ] Any certifications (ISO, BIS, etc.) and customer names or counts.
- [ ] A PDF catalogue, if one should be linked (`site.catalogueUrl` in `content/site.ts`).
- [ ] Permission to publish the supplied logo and product images from setmiindia.com.
